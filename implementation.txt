ML (FROM SCRATCH, NumPy) — Rubric priority. Each model needs a baseline, a proper split, metrics, and an honest evaluation.
1. Priority scoring: a transparent weighted formula (severity × frequency × recency decay × traffic/context exposure × complaint density × citizen trust) PLUS a learned component. Weights are shown in the UI and adjustable by the admin. Output is a score, a confidence, and the top contributing reasons. Confidence should come from data volume, agreement between sources and recency.
2. Resolution-time regressor: gradient-boosted regression trees implemented from scratch (histogram-based splits, MSE loss, shrinkage, early stopping), with a Ridge regression from scratch as a second model. Baseline: per-category median. Metrics: MAE, RMSE, and calibration of prediction intervals (quantile or residual-based). Time-aware split.
3. Incident forecaster: per location and per week (or month) counts predicted by Poisson regression from scratch (gradient descent) with lag, seasonality and complaint-lag features. Baseline: last-period and moving average. Metrics: MAE, Poisson deviance, and top-k hit rate (of the 50 locations we ranked highest, how many actually had incidents next period). Strict time-aware split, no leakage.
4. Complaint text classifier: TF-IDF built from scratch plus multinomial softmax regression from scratch (and a Naive Bayes baseline). Metrics: macro-F1 and a confusion matrix. Used for category/severity suggestions in the citizen app. If the complaints data has no free text, derive the best available signal and say so honestly.
5. Anomaly detector: seasonal EWMA plus a robust z-score (median/MAD) per zone and category, with an evaluation using injected synthetic spikes (recall and false-alarm rate, clearly labeled as synthetic injection).
6. Budget optimizer: 0/1 knapsack DP and a greedy comparison, reporting the gain over the naive baseline.
